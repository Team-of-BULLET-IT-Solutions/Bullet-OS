Bullet-OS with Apple iOS Experience

Bullet-OS is an operating system created exclusively by BULLET IT Solutions. It combines the world-famous elegance, glassmorphism, Dynamic Island, and gestures of Apple iOS with an open, high-performance Linux Kernel 6.1 & AOSP foundation.

  • 🏝️
    Bullet Island: Pill-shaped interactive cutout that morphs for music playback, Bullet Boost mode, and call notifications.
  • 🎛️
    Bullet Control Center: Swipe or tap top-right to reveal authentic iOS blurred sliders for brightness, volume, and connectivity.
  • 📱
    Apple iOS Squircles & Floating Dock: 22.5% continuous curvature superellipse icons with a frosted glass dock.
  • ⚡
    Bullet Lab & Shield: Native C++ governor for gaming boost and strict sensor hardware kill-switch.
  • 📦
    100% Android App Compatibility: Runs any Android APK natively with pure iOS fluidity.
💡 Interactive Gestures to Test on the Phone:
• Click the Bullet Island (black pill at top) to expand music player.
• Click Top-Right (Battery/5G) to pull down the iOS Control Center.
• Click Top-Left (Time) to view the Lock Screen & Notifications.
• Click any App Icon (Settings, Terminal, Phone, Notes) to launch.
• Click the Home Bar at the bottom to exit back to Home.

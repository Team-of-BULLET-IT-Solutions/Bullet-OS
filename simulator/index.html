Bullet-OS • AquaFlow Design

Bullet-OS is an independent, next-generation mobile operating system engineered entirely by Team BULLET IT Solutions. Powered by the AquaFlow UI Engine and the Linux Kernel 6.1 foundation, it delivers nature-inspired fluidity, ultra-low latency, and modern flagship elegance.

  • 💧
    AquaFlow Visual System: Fluid organic motion, water-inspired color hierarchy, pebble squircle icons, and blurred multi-layer translucent glass.
  • ⚡
    Bullet Capsule: Smart top-status capsule that seamlessly expands for live audio, battery charging, and Bullet Boost notifications.
  • 🎛️
    Dual-Tile Quick Center: Dual prominent network cards for Wi-Fi and 5G cellular, with fluid horizontal brightness and audio sliders.
  • 🏎️
    Bullet Performance Engine (`bulletd`): Native C++ governor daemon enabling dynamic CPU clock boosting and 120Hz HyperMotion.
  • 🛡️
    Bullet Privacy Shield: Real-time hardware microphone and camera sensor isolation with kernel-level access guarding.
💡 Interactive Simulator Controls:
• Tap the Bullet Capsule (top pill) to expand the music player.
• Tap Top-Right (Battery/5G) to pull down the Quick Center.
• Tap Top-Left (Time) to view the Lock Screen & Notifications.
• Tap Any App Icon (Settings, Terminal, Phone, Notes) to launch.
• Tap the Gesture Bar at the bottom to exit back to Home.
